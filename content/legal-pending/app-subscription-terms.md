## What the terms will cover

- The SiteGuard App trade packages and builder bundles, and SiteGuard Audit seats.
- Billing: annual billing is the default; paying every 6 months, every 3 months or monthly adds a loading. Prices are in Australian dollars and exclude GST; GST is added at checkout.
- Renewal: plans renew automatically at the end of each period unless cancelled before then, with a reminder before an annual plan renews.
- Price changes: prices may rise once a year, with notice.
- Cancellation and refunds, alongside the Refunds and Cancellations Policy.
- The documents included in each package are templates: the business using them reviews and adapts each one to its own work and site.
