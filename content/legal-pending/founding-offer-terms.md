## The offer

- **Who:** the first 20 businesses to buy a founding place.
- **What:** 12 months of the SiteGuard App: a trade package (Domestic, Light Commercial or Heavy Commercial), a builder bundle (Domestic, Light Commercial or Heavy Commercial) or SiteGuard Audit seats.
- **Price:** 15% off the standard annual price for the first 12 months. Prices are in Australian dollars and exclude GST; GST is added at checkout.
- **Payment:** annually, up front, at purchase.
- **Access:** starts when the app opens, planned for June 2027. That is an expected date, not a promise. The 12 months run from the day the app opens.
- **Price lock:** the founding price applies for those 12 months only. After that the standard price applies, and the plan renews annually unless you cancel before it renews. We remind you before it renews.
- **While you wait:** the matching SiteGuard FORMS pack is included free from March 2027.
- **If the opening date moves:** one free month of access for each month of delay, or, on request, a refund of the unused prepayment.
