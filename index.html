ISO 45001 / 9001 / 14001 — System Development Support
Gap analysis, system design, policy and procedure development, and evidence preparation to support your organisation's journey towards ISO. Delivered as standalone standard frameworks or combined into a single Integrated Management System — one framework, one audit cycle, supporting all three standards.
Integrated Management Systems (IMS)
A single structured framework combining ISO 45001, 9001 and 14001 — removing the duplication of running three separate systems. One policy structure, one risk register, one internal audit cycle. SiteGuard designs and builds the IMS framework; your organisation implements and maintains it.
Psychosocial Hazards — ISO 45003:2021
Positive duty on psychosocial hazard management is now explicit under Australian OHS and WHS legislation and enforced by state regulators since 2023. SiteGuard develops ISO 45003-aligned frameworks covering hazard identification, risk controls, consultation structures and evidence records — supporting your organisation in meeting its obligations.

About

Daniel Silver

Founder & Principal HSEQ Consultant — SiteGuard Systems

Daniel is a senior HSEQ practitioner with over 27 years in the construction industry. He has worked across Tier 2 and Tier 3 builders, large subcontractors and complex civil and commercial projects — building management systems from the ground up and supporting companies on their journey towards ISO and OHS/WHS management system improvement.

SiteGuard was founded on a simple observation: construction companies are often buried in compliance paperwork but short on practical systems that hold up on site. Every document and service SiteGuard provides is built to solve a real operational problem — not to fill a folder.

The approach is straightforward: senior practitioner delivery, plain-English documents, and systems designed to be used on site, not filed and forgotten.
